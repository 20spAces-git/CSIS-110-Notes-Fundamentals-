# 🤖 Emerging Technologies & Artificial Intelligence

Technology is constantly changing. New tools and systems are being developed that can change how people work, communicate, travel, learn, and solve problems.

Some of these newer technologies are called **emerging technologies**.

In this lesson, I am learning about emerging technologies such as **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, the Internet of Things (IoT), Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), robotics, and cloud computing.**

---

## 📖 What Are Emerging Technologies?

**Emerging technologies** are technologies that are new, rapidly developing, or becoming more widely used.

Some technologies may have existed for years but are considered emerging because they are becoming more powerful or are being used in new ways.

Examples include:

- 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
- 🧠 Machine Learning
- 🥽 Virtual Reality
- 📱 Augmented Reality
- 🌐 Internet of Things
- 🤖 Robotics
- ☁️ Cloud Computing

These technologies are becoming increasingly common in everyday life.

---

## 🖼️ What Does Emerging Technology Look Like?

It can be something as simple as:

- A smartwatch tracking your activity

# 🤖 Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## 📖 What Is Artificial Intelligence?

**Artificial Intelligence**, usually called **AI**, is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require some form of human intelligence.

These tasks can include:

- Recognizing images
- Understanding language
- Finding patterns
- Making predictions
- Recommending content
- Generating text or images
- Helping make decisions

AI does not necessarily "think" the same way a human does.

Instead, AI systems use **data, algorithms, and computing power** to recognize patterns and produce results.

---

## 💡 Simple Example

Imagine teaching someone to recognize a dog.

You might show them many pictures of dogs.

🐕 Labrador  
🐩 Poodle  
🐕 German Shepherd  
🐶 Puppy  

Eventually, they begin recognizing similarities between the pictures.

Some AI systems learn patterns in a somewhat similar way.

They can be trained using large amounts of **data** so that they can recognize patterns and make predictions when they receive new information.

---

## ⚙️ What Does AI Actually Do?

A simplified AI process can look like this:

**Data → Processing → Pattern Recognition → Result**

For example:

A system may receive thousands of email messages.

It analyzes patterns found in known spam messages.

Then a new email arrives.

The system analyzes that email and may predict:

> ⚠️ This message is probably spam.

The AI did not magically know the answer.

It used patterns learned from data to make a prediction.

---

# 🧠 What Is Machine Learning?

**Machine Learning (ML)** is an area of Artificial Intelligence where computer systems learn patterns from data.

Instead of programming every possible answer manually, developers can provide data that helps the system recognize patterns.

### Example

A bank might use machine learning to help detect unusual transactions.

If a system notices behavior that is very different from normal activity, it might flag the transaction for additional review.

Machine learning is used in areas such as:

- Fraud detection
- Cybersecurity
- Recommendation systems
- Healthcare
- Speech recognition
- Image recognition

---

# ✨ What Is Generative AI?

**Generative AI** is a type of AI designed to generate new content based on patterns learned from training data.

It can generate things such as:

- 📝 Text
- 🖼️ Images
- 🎵 Audio
- 🎥 Video
- 💻 Computer code

Examples include AI assistants that can answer questions, summarize information, help brainstorm ideas, or generate images.

Generative AI can be useful, but its answers should still be reviewed.

AI can sometimes produce information that is incorrect, incomplete, or misleading.

# 🌎 AI in the Real World

AI is already being used in many industries.

## 🏥 Healthcare

AI can help analyze medical information, organize data, and assist with identifying patterns in medical images.

Human healthcare professionals still make important medical decisions.

---

## 🔐 Cybersecurity

AI can help cybersecurity professionals analyze large amounts of information.

For example, AI may help identify:

- Suspicious network activity
- Malware patterns
- Unusual login attempts
- Phishing messages
- Possible fraudulent activity

AI can help analysts find suspicious behavior faster, but humans are still needed to investigate and make decisions.

---

## 🚗 Transportation

Modern vehicles may use:

- Cameras
- Radar
- Sensors
- GPS
- Computer systems
- AI

These technologies can help with features such as lane detection, collision warnings, parking assistance, and navigation.

---

## 🎮 Gaming

AI has been used in video games for many years.

It can control:

- Enemies
- Teammates
- Non-player characters (NPCs)
- Difficulty
- Character behavior

More advanced AI systems can also help developers create or test game content.

---

# 🌐 Internet of Things (IoT)

The **Internet of Things**, or **IoT**, describes physical devices that can connect to networks and exchange information.

Examples include:

- ⌚ Smartwatches
- 📺 Smart TVs
- 🔔 Smart doorbells
- 💡 Smart lights
- 🌡️ Smart thermostats
- 📷 Security cameras

For example, a smart thermostat may connect to Wi-Fi so the homeowner can control the temperature from a smartphone.

---

## 🔐 Why IoT Security Matters

Connecting devices to a network can create additional security risks.

An IoT device with:

- A weak password
- Outdated software
- Poor security settings

could potentially become a security weakness.

This is why cybersecurity also matters for devices that may not look like traditional computers.

---

# 🥽 Virtual Reality (VR)

**Virtual Reality**, or **VR**, places the user inside a computer-generated environment.

A VR headset can make the user feel like they are inside:

- A video game
- A training simulation
- A virtual classroom
- A digital workspace

VR can be used for entertainment, education, training, and simulations.

---

# 📱 Augmented Reality (AR)

**Augmented Reality**, or **AR**, adds digital information to the real world.

AR does not completely replace what the user sees.

Instead, it adds digital objects or information on top of the real environment.

### Example

A smartphone camera might show the room around you while placing a digital object on the screen.

One easy way to remember the difference is:

> 🥽 **VR replaces your environment.**
>
> 📱 **AR adds to your environment.**

---

# 🤖 Robotics

**Robotics** involves designing and using machines that can perform physical tasks.

Robots can be found in:

- 🏭 Factories
- 📦 Warehouses
- 🏥 Hospitals
- 🚀 Space exploration
- 🏠 Homes

Some robots can perform repetitive tasks automatically.

Others may use sensors, cameras, programming, and AI to respond to their surroundings.

---

# ☁️ Cloud Computing

**Cloud computing** allows people and organizations to use computing resources over a network, usually the Internet.

Instead of everything being stored or processed only on your own computer, some resources can exist on remote computers called **servers**.

Examples include:

- Online file storage
- Web-based email
- Streaming services
- Online applications
- Game saves
- Business systems

A simple way to think about it is:

> ☁️ **Your computer uses resources located somewhere else through a network.**

---

# ⚠️ Benefits and Risks of Emerging Technology

Emerging technology can provide many benefits.

### ✅ Possible Benefits

- Faster communication
- Automation of repetitive tasks
- Better accessibility
- Improved data analysis
- New educational tools
- Improved safety systems
- New career opportunities

However, new technology can also create challenges.

### ⚠️ Possible Risks

- Privacy concerns
- Cybersecurity threats
- Incorrect AI-generated information
- Bias in AI systems
- Job responsibilities changing because of automation
- Overdependence on technology
- Misuse of personal information

Understanding both the benefits and risks is important.

---

# 🔏 AI, Data, and Privacy

AI systems often depend on **data**.

That makes privacy an important concern.

Information entered into an online service may potentially include:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Photos
- Documents
- Location information
- School information
- Workplace information

Before entering information into an AI system or other online service, users should think about whether the information is private or sensitive.

### 🔐 Beginner Safety Rule

> **Do not enter passwords, Social Security numbers, private financial information, confidential work information, or other sensitive personal information into an AI tool.**

# 🧑‍💻 Will AI Replace Jobs?

AI and automation may change how some jobs are performed.

Some tasks may become automated while new types of jobs may also be created.

For IT professionals, understanding AI may become another useful skill.

People may increasingly work **with AI tools** rather than simply being replaced by them.

Human skills such as:

- Critical thinking
- Communication
- Problem solving
- Creativity
- Decision making

remain important.

# 🛠️ Why Emerging Technology Matters in IT

IT professionals need to understand new technologies because they may eventually be responsible for:

- Installing them
- Configuring them
- Troubleshooting them
- Securing them
- Supporting users
- Managing their data
- Connecting them to networks

Technology changes quickly.

Learning the fundamentals makes it easier to understand newer technologies later.

# 🔗 Connection to Previous Lessons

Emerging technologies connect many of the concepts I have already learned in CSIS 110.

🧠 **RAM** temporarily stores information programs are actively using.

⚙️ **CPU** processes instructions needed to run software and technology.

💾 **Storage** keeps applications, files, and data.

⌨️🖥️ **Input and Output Devices** allow users and computer systems to exchange information.

🧩 **Motherboards** connect important computer components.

🪟 **Operating Systems** manage hardware and allow applications to run.

📦 **Software** gives computers instructions for performing tasks.

🌐 **Networking** allows devices and systems to communicate.

🔐 **Cybersecurity** protects systems, networks, devices, and information.

🗄️ **Data and Databases** help organize the information many modern technologies depend on.

💻 **Programming** provides instructions that allow software and computer systems to perform tasks.

Emerging technologies often combine several of these concepts together.
